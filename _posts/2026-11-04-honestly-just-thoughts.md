[https://mindingourway.com/dive-in-2/](https://mindingourway.com/dive-in-2/) - Bob and Alice. Generator will help me be Alice

The only way to get a good model of the world inside your head is to [bump into the world](https://mindingourway.com/what-sort-of-thing-a-brain-is/), to let the light and sound impinge upon your eyes and ears, and let the world carve the details into your world-model. Similarly, the only method I know of for finding actual good plans is to take a bad plan and *slam it into the world,* to let evidence and the feedback impinge upon your strategy, and let the world tell you where the better ideas are.
