11/16

At Copenhagen Airport right now. Reading about the country, fun things I learned:

- Hygge - the famous Danish word for the pleasure of settling in with good company and simple comforts. Think friends around a candlelit dinner table on a dark November evening.
- biggest international businesses include pharmaceuticals (Novo Nordisk, maker of Ozempic and Wegovy), shipping and logistics (Maersk), and wind energy
- 1/3 people work in government 
- King Harald Bluetooth - our phone’s Bluetooth is named after him
- It still has a monarchy today (Frederik X became king in January 2024) 
- *Janteloven*, the “Law of Jante” - don’t think you’re better than everyone else
- *fællesspisning*, communal dinners where you can sit at a long table with neighbors or strangers
- only 6 million people 
- if you turn 25 and you’re unmarried, your friends might cover you in cinnamon
- 37-hour workweek is standard
- social trust is high
- it can take a while to break into established friend groups
- in the 1960s, Danish factories needed workers, and people came from Turkey, Pakistan, and what was then Yugoslavia. Denmark halted that kind of recruitment in 1973 after the oil crisis
