*There are a few questions I want to revisit every few months, and this is one of them. I want to do this for two reasons (1) to see how my answers change, what evidence changed my mind, and reflect on whether those changes should affect what I choose to work on, and (2) to leave a trail for someone who might have a similar journey.*

*There's so much context you need when you're first learning about AI safety. Some questions you have were discussed years ago, others are based on misunderstandings you don't yet have the knowledge to recognize. Newcomers (like me) can't easily reconstruct the path to those conclusions and I think experts often forget what it was like not to know what they know. I want to document that process while I'm still going through it, before I forget what it was like to be new to the field. I wish I could have seen more of what people in the field believed and questioned when they were first starting out.*
